About this calculator

Discount Calculator answers two questions shoppers ask all the time: what will I pay, and how much am I saving?

Enter a price and a discount to get the sale price. Add a second discount for "extra % off at checkout" deals, and add sales tax to see the amount you'll actually be charged. If a shop shows only an old and a new price, switch to Percent off to find the real discount.

How it works

All the maths happens in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere. Stacked discounts are applied one after another, and tax is applied last, the same way a till rings up a sale.
